## Fonts

Instrument Serif regular and italic are locally hosted WOFF2 editions of the Google Fonts distribution. The accompanying SIL Open Font License is in `assets/fonts/Instrument-Serif-OFL.txt`. [Instrument Serif source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif)

DM Sans is locally hosted as a variable WOFF2. The accompanying SIL Open Font License is in `assets/fonts/DM-Sans-OFL.txt`. [DM Sans source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans)

## Identity

The abstract bird mark in assets/icons/aerelle.svg was drawn originally for this project. The same mark appears in the header, drawer, footer and favicon. AERELLE is a proposed working brand. Trademark and domain availability have not been verified.
