Frequently asked questions

Have a reference photo or an occasion in mind? Include the details in your notes.

Is my preferred time confirmed?

The form prepares a request on your device. Contact the salon directly to agree on a time.

How is the price agreed?

Menu prices are indicative. Discuss length, products and styling needs with the salon before your appointment.

Can I change my treatment preference?

Change the category or service before reviewing. Use Edit details to return to the form and adjust your selection.

What can I discuss at a consultation?

Share your routine, preferred finish, inspiration images and any sensitivities. Use a menu service as a starting point for the conversation.

How do I prepare a request?

Choose your treatment, add your contact details and preferred time, then review the summary. Download it and contact the salon directly to arrange confirmation.
