Before your appointment

A few useful answers.

Plan the details before you prepare your request.

How do I choose a treatment?

Start with the service category closest to what you have in mind. The Services page explains each treatment and its approximate duration.

Can I discuss my preferred result?

Include a reference, a preferred finish or details you would like to change in the notes on your request.

Are the menu prices final?

Prices are indicative starting amounts in INR. Agree on the final price with the salon after discussing your service requirements.

How do I prepare an appointment request?

Choose a service, add your contact details and preferred time, then review and download your request. Share it with the salon to arrange confirmation.
